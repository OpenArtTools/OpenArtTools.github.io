# Privacy

Open Art Tools — concept, idea, design, creation & development by Gerard Valls Montaño.

Open Art Tools is a **web platform that hosts tools**. It does **not** store your personal or contract data on servers or in the browser.

## Absolute rule: nothing is stored by the platform

Open Art Tools **does not store any of your data**:

- No cloud accounts
- No analytics or trackers
- No servers that receive form contents
- No `localStorage`, cookies, or on-device vaults controlled by the platform
- No client agendas or third-party contact databases
- Form data lives **only in memory** while the tab is open

**Closing the tab clears it from the platform.** A persistent strip reminds you of this. While you have a document in progress, you can download its draft from that strip.

## Optional files you control

You can download and later reload two kinds of `.json` files. Both stay wherever **you** save them. Open Art Tools never uploads them and never keeps a copy.

### 1. Personal profile (`openarttools.profile`) — platform level

Your own identity data (name, document, address, email, phone…). Managed on the **platform home**. Designed to be reused in **any tool**. It is **not** a list of clients.

### 2. Document draft (`openarttools.draft`) — inside each tool

A snapshot of one agreement (form values, clauses, and edit state). Lives **inside the tool** (e.g. exhibition agreements). You can download it anytime while that document is in memory.

You are responsible for how you store and share those files.

## What else leaves the platform

Only if **you** choose to export: PDF / HTML / TXT of the document. The platform does not upload those files.

## In the product

The UI always shows transparency and session strips. The **Transparencia** page repeats these promises and links to this file and `AUDITABILITY.md`.
